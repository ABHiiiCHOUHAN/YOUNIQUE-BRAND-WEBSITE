================================================================================
YOUNIQUE STUDIO — RECOMMENDED IMAGE SIZES CHEAT SHEET
================================================================================

Place your images into their respective folders below.
Follow these recommended dimensions and formats for optimal quality and speed (<0.4s load):

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WORK SECTION (/work and /work/[id])
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Folder: static/images/work/

Subfolders:
├── branding/     → Brand identity assets, logos, collateral
├── digital/      → Web and mobile UI/UX layouts
├── social/       → Social media campaigns and posts
├── video/        → Motion graphics, video stills, storyboards
├── graphics/     → Print, posters, editorial spreads
├── models/       → 3D renders, CGI passes, product visuals
└── packaging/    → Box mockups, labels, dielines

Recommended Sizes:
• Project Cover (Thumbnail & Deck Banner):
  - Size: 1600 × 900 px (16:9) or 1200 × 800 px (3:2)
  - Format: WebP or JPG
  - Max Weight: < 200 KB

• Gallery Wide Slide:
  - Size: 1600 × 1000 px (16:10)
  - Format: WebP or JPG
  - Max Weight: < 300 KB

• Gallery Square Slide (Details / Icons):
  - Size: 1000 × 1000 px (1:1)
  - Format: WebP, PNG, or JPG
  - Max Weight: < 200 KB

• Desktop Long-Scroll Mockup (Digital Deck):
  - Size: 1200 × 2400 px (1:2)
  - Format: WebP or PNG
  - Max Weight: < 450 KB

• Mobile Long-Scroll Mockup (Digital Deck):
  - Size: 600 × 1800 px (1:3)
  - Format: WebP or PNG
  - Max Weight: < 250 KB

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BLOG SECTION (/blog and /blog/[id])
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Folder: static/images/blog/

Subfolders:
├── covers/       → Featured covers for blog cards and hero headers
└── articles/     → In-article screenshots, charts, and diagrams

Recommended Sizes:
• Blog Card & Hero Cover:
  - Size: 1200 × 675 px (16:9)
  - Format: WebP or JPG
  - Max Weight: < 180 KB

• In-Article Figure (Wide):
  - Size: 1200 × 700 px
  - Format: WebP or PNG
  - Max Weight: < 200 KB

• In-Article Diagram (Square):
  - Size: 800 × 800 px (1:1)
  - Format: SVG or WebP
  - Max Weight: < 150 KB

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOME PAGE (Landing & Sections)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Folder: static/images/home/

Recommended Sizes:
• Hero Background: 1920 × 1080 px (16:9) | WebP / AVIF | < 300 KB
• Service Stickers: 512 × 512 px (1:1) | Transparent PNG / SVG | < 100 KB
• Review Avatars: 400 × 400 px (1:1 circular) | WebP / JPG | < 80 KB

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ABOUT PAGE (/about)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Folder: static/images/about/

Recommended Sizes:
• Studio Hero Banner: 1200 × 800 px (3:2) | WebP / JPG | < 250 KB
• Team Member Portrait: 600 × 800 px (3:4) | WebP / JPG | < 150 KB
